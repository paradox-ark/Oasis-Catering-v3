Our Story

From a Kuri Road kitchen to Islamabad's grandest feasts.

Oasis began in 1988 with a single deg, fresh local spices, and an uncompromising standard: genuine hospitality cooked from the heart. Over thirty-five years, that passion has grown into one of Islamabad and Rawalpindi's most trusted full-service catering and event houses.

Whether you are planning an intimate rooftop birthday or a 3,000-guest wedding banquet, you deal directly with the family responsible for your food quality, marquee safety, and guest satisfaction.
