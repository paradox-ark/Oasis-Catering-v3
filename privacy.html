Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Oasis Catering and Event Management (“Oasis”, “we”) respects your privacy. This website requires no mandatory login, user profiling or tracking cookies.

1. Information You Share With Us

When you request a quotation or message us via WhatsApp or Email, you voluntarily share details such as your name, contact phone number, event date, guest count, and culinary preferences. This transmission occurs directly through your own communication client (WhatsApp or email client) so our event managers can respond accurately.

2. Purpose & Use of Data

  • We use your contact and event details solely to prepare custom menu proposals, calculate quotes, and coordinate logistics on event day.
  • We never sell, rent, or distribute client contact information to advertisers, telemarketers, or external third parties.
  • Inquiries are retained only as long as needed to fulfill your event consultation and subsequent booking records.

3. Client Accounts & Local Storage

We do not operate password-protected customer databases or member portals. Your menu shortlist is stored exclusively in your own browser's LocalStorage for convenience and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

4. External Services & Embedded Media

Our website utilizes industry-standard infrastructure including Google Fonts, Google Maps (location frame), and direct links to Meta properties (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook). These external providers operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Rights & Contact

To modify, update, or request deletion of any event communication records, simply email oasiscatering.pk@gmail.com or speak directly with Waqar Ahmed at 0329 5977659.
